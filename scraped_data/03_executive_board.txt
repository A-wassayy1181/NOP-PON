BOARD RESPONSIBILITIES
----------------------
The Executive Board oversees party operations including:

- Developing and guiding party policies and initiatives
- Organizing elections, campaigns, and events
- Supporting riding associations and local representatives
- Managing membership engagement and growth
- Ensuring transparency, accountability, and effective governance


MISSION & VISION
----------------
"To be the voice of Northern Ontario — empowering our communities and
protecting our resources and to create a strong, self-reliant Northern
Ontario where decisions are made by the people who live here, for the
people who live here."
